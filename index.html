Behind the timeline / 02

Hi, I'm Allen.

I’m Allen James, a video editor and motion graphics artist with over four years of experience creating content for digital platforms and a range of industries. I specialize in short-form reels, cinematic trailers, motion graphics, ads, and podcast content—combining thoughtful pacing and visual detail to help each story connect with its audience.

What I do

I’ve edited ads, organic social videos, UGC, testimonials, long- and short-form podcasts, public talks, course materials, and gaming montages. I also have experience in Live2D avatar rigging and graphic design.

How I work

I adapt the editing style to the audience, brand voice, and platform. I’m comfortable managing multiple projects, collaborating with clients, and delivering consistent work to deadlines.
